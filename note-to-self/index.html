How it works

Note to Self

Chat screenshot preview
  1. Type a message — or add your own auto-replies first (see Auto-Replies below) so a keyword sends yourself a word back.
  2. Pick a time with the clock to schedule a message to your future self (green bubbles). With saving off, keep this tab open; with saving on, overdue notes arrive when you reopen it.
  3. Tap Draw to sketch a message, and schedule it too if you like.

Auto-Replies

Auto-Replies screenshot preview
  1. Open Note to Self and tap the speech-bubble icon in the bottom bar to open Auto-Replies.
  2. Choose Text for keyword replies or Drawing for replies to a drawing type you select.
  3. Enter keywords or drawing types, separated by commas, then write the message you want to receive.
  4. Tap Add auto-reply, then Save. Send a matching text message, or choose that type when sending a drawing, to receive your reply.

Your privacy

Close the tab and it's gone — unless you choose to save. By default, your messages, drawings, scheduled notes and profile photo disappear when you close or reload the tab. Turn on "Save messages on this device" to keep them in this browser. Turning it off deletes the saved copy, while your current chat stays until you leave.

  • Messages, drawings, profile photos and audio are never sent to any service. Optional saved notes stay only in this browser, not in an account or on our servers. Anyone using this browser can access saved notes; clearing browser data removes them.
  • Your auto-reply keywords and reply text are kept in this browser's storage on your device, independently of the message-saving toggle — nothing leaves it.
  • No accounts, no tracking, no analytics.
